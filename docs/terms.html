2. Description of Service

VeriCapture is a media verification application that creates SHA-256 hashes of photos and videos, and records those hashes on the Solana blockchain as immutable proof that a specific file existed in a specific form at a specific time.

Key features include:

  • SHA-256 hashing of photos and videos captured on or stored on your device
  • Two-stage hash chain: Stage 1 commits the content hash on-chain, Stage 2 commits a sealed hash for tamper detection
  • Media verification history with on-chain transaction references
  • Optional wallet connection for Seeker platform identity and SGT verification
  • Free capture, hashing, proof recording and verification for everyone — you pay only the Solana network fee for each on-chain transaction
  • Optional one-time VeriCapture Pro purchase (0.01 SOL or 10 SKR) unlocking video proof, unlimited proof history, auto-prove on capture, and certificate export; free for Seeker Genesis Token holders

The App NEVER uploads your photos or videos. Only cryptographic hashes are created and recorded on-chain. Your media files remain solely on your device at all times.

3. Nature of Media Hashes

You acknowledge and agree that:

  • SHA-256 hashes are one-way cryptographic functions — the original media file cannot be reconstructed from the hash
  • Hashes recorded on the Solana blockchain are immutable and permanent — they cannot be edited, deleted, or removed once confirmed on-chain
  • A hash proves that a specific file existed in a specific form at a specific time, but it does not prove who took the photo or video, where it was taken, or that the file was not modified before hashing
  • Two-stage verification (Stage 1: content hash, Stage 2: sealed hash) provides tamper detection for the hashed file, but does not verify the authenticity of the original capture event
  • On-chain hashes are not legal evidence in any jurisdiction unless specifically accepted as such by the relevant authority
  • The accuracy of hashes depends on the integrity of the media file at the time of hashing
  • Proof recording is subject to Solana network availability and confirmation times
