VeriCapture — Disclaimer

Effective: March 2026

WARNING: VeriCapture creates SHA-256 hashes of your photos and videos and records them on the Solana blockchain. On-chain hashes are IMMUTABLE and CANNOT be deleted once recorded. VeriCapture does NOT upload your media files. On-chain hashes do NOT constitute legal proof of media authenticity in any jurisdiction. VeriCapture is NOT a forensic or legal tool. You use VeriCapture entirely at your own risk.

1. Nature of the Application

VeriCapture ("the App") is a media verification tool designed for the Solana Seeker device. The App creates SHA-256 hashes of photos and videos using a two-stage hash chain (Stage 1 = content hash of the media file, Stage 2 = sealed hash for tamper detection) and records those hashes on the Solana blockchain via the Memo Program.

  • VeriCapture does not upload, transmit, or store your photos, videos, or any media files to any server, blockchain, or third party.
  • VeriCapture does not constitute legal evidence, legal proof, or official documentation of media authenticity in any jurisdiction.
  • VeriCapture is not a forensic, legal, or investigative tool.
  • On-chain hashes are immutable and cannot be edited, deleted, or removed once confirmed on the Solana blockchain.

2. Hash Immutability

Once a SHA-256 hash is recorded on the Solana blockchain, it is permanent and irreversible. You acknowledge and accept that:

  • There is no mechanism to delete, modify, or redact an on-chain hash after confirmation
  • MidMightBit Games cannot remove or alter on-chain hashes on your behalf or at your request
  • You are solely responsible for deciding which media files to hash before confirming the on-chain transaction
  • Hash immutability is a fundamental characteristic of blockchain technology, not a defect

Consider carefully before recording hashes on-chain. Once confirmed, they exist permanently on the public Solana blockchain.

3. Not Legal Evidence

On-chain media hashes created by VeriCapture are not legal evidence. You acknowledge that:

  • Hashes may not be admissible in any court, tribunal, arbitration, or legal proceeding
  • Hashes prove only that a specific media file produced a specific hash output at a specific time — they do not independently verify the authenticity, origin, or unaltered nature of the underlying photo or video
  • Hashes do not replace professional forensic analysis, chain-of-custody documentation, or expert authentication of media files
  • The admissibility and weight of blockchain records varies by jurisdiction and is not guaranteed
  • VeriCapture is not a substitute for professional legal, forensic, or investigative services

You should not rely on VeriCapture as your sole or primary method of proving the authenticity of photos or videos for legal, regulatory, or compliance purposes.

4. Media Accuracy

The accuracy of media hashes depends entirely on the media file itself at the time of hashing. VeriCapture hashes the file as-is and does not independently verify the accuracy or authenticity of the source media. You acknowledge that:

  • VeriCapture does not verify whether a photo or video was altered, edited, or manipulated before hashing
  • VeriCapture does not verify the origin, creator, or capture device of any media file
  • VeriCapture does not verify EXIF metadata, GPS coordinates, or timestamps embedded in media files
  • A valid on-chain hash proves only that the file existed in its hashed form at the time of recording — it does not prove the file is an unmodified original

MidMightBit Games makes no representations about the accuracy, completeness, authenticity, or reliability of any media file hashed through the App.
